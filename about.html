ABOUT PATCHKITE

Website quality, always on watch.

Patchkite is built around a simple idea: website problems should be discovered before visitors have to discover them.

THE IDEA

Quiet problems deserve attention.

A website can look perfectly fine while individual links or resources quietly stop working. Patchkite is designed to make those problems easier to discover.
