What does Patchkite check?

Patchkite checks important website resources and reports problems that may affect visitors.

01

Broken Links

Patchkite checks links discovered on the website and identifies links that fail to respond correctly.

02

Missing Images

Patchkite checks image resources and helps identify images that cannot be loaded.

03

Website Resources

Patchkite examines resources used by the page while performing a scan.

04

HTTP Responses

Patchkite records HTTP response information when checking website resources.
